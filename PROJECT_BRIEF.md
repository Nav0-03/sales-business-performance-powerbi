# UK Economic Indicators Dashboard - Project Brief

## Business Problem
UK policymakers, economists, and businesses need to understand economic health 
across regions. This dashboard provides accessible, actionable insights into:
- Employment trends
- Economic growth
- Cost of living
- Real wage development
- Regional disparities

## Target Users
- Policy analysts
- Economics students
- Business strategists
- Media/journalists covering economy

## Key Questions to Answer
1. How has unemployment changed by region since 2020?
2. Is real wage growth keeping up with inflation?
3. Which regions are growing fastest?
4. What's the correlation between GDP growth and employment?
5. How do regions compare on key metrics?
